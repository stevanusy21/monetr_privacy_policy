Privacy Policy for Monetr

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Monetr is a 100% offline personal expense and money tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial data.

  • All transactions, debts, subscriptions, accounts, and categories you enter are stored locally and privately on your device.
  • The app does not connect to the internet or any external servers.
  • Exported files (Excel / JSON backup) are generated locally and only shared when you explicitly choose to do so via your device's native share sheet.
  • We have no access to the financial records you enter into the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Monetr does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or cloud backup services.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete your records at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset All Data" option within the app's Settings menu.
  • Clearing the app's storage in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.
